Last Z Volcano War — Schedule and Battle Plan

Event Guide • Updated September 8, 2026 • Last Z Guides

Quick Answer

Volcano War’s battle is on Day 6 at 12:00 Apocalypse Time and lasts one hour. Your President arranges camp partners and spoil shares before combat; your alliance assigns objectives. You need HQ 15 to enter, and building battles can kill troops. Fight for Camp Points while tracking your personal reward eligibility.

Invitations, Hard Mode, and the Reward Pool

A state can have one outgoing invitation at a time. It expires after one hour without a response. Arrange availability and agree the spoil split before sending it; the agreed shares determine distribution.

Defenders choose Hard Mode during Comms. It prevents them from inviting allied states, while the attacking camp can contain up to three states. The Defender receives a combat buff, but must weigh it against fighting without invited partners.

The reward-pool panel lists successful invitation ×2, victory ×2, and Defender Hard Mode ×3. These are pool multipliers, not a promise that every player receives that multiplier on every reward. Check the displayed pool and negotiated shares before choosing a camp.

States assigned automatically by the system do not increase the reward pool and are not eligible for its spoil distribution.

Entry, Teleporting, and Troop Losses

  • HQ 15 or higher is required. The battle takes place in the Defender state.
  • HQs already inside the combat area are moved out when combat begins. Teleport back in after the start.
  • Entering from outside after the start does not consume teleporters. Moving within the combat area is free with a 5-minute cooldown. Volcano War, Alliance Duel, and State Duel share cross-state teleport cooldowns.
  • The central zone blocks teleporting and Peace Shields.
  • Building battles have a 10% Seriously Injured Rate and a 10% Mortality Rate. Plan for troop losses; these figures apply to the event’s building battles.

Buildings: Camp Points and Personal Points

The camp with the most Camp Points wins. Personal Points track your own contribution. The listed building rates are separate scoring entries; they do not mean every camp member receives the personal rate.

Each survivor can send one team per building; a building holds up to ten teams. Entry prioritizes the strongest teams by remaining units and power. When several alliances compete, each alliance’s top ten teams count toward Comprehensive Power, and the highest total gets capture priority.

Collecting Rewards After Combat

Personal ranking rewards require at least 2,000 Contribution. This eligibility floor is separate from the camp’s winning score.

Camp win/loss rewards, personal ranking rewards, and distributed spoils are separate reward screens. Check each one. Spoils are distributed after combat: Presidents act on Days 7–8, then R4/R5 on Days 9–10. Each alliance can receive up to 100 spoil shares; each survivor can receive only one.

The schedule, scoring, and entry rules above match Server 403’s September 2026 event. Check your event’s Hard Mode and reward panels before applying them to a different round.
