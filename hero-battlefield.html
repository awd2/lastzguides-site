Last Z Hero Battlefield — Resets, Replays, and Quick Fight

Hero Progression Guide • Updated September 7, 2026 • Last Z Guides

Quick Answer

Use each available Hero Battlefield every Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday session. A hero can enter only one battlefield per session. If the next chapter defeats your team, replay a completed chapter for rewards. Fully clearing Blood Rose Lv. 20 unlocks Quick Fight.

Three Battlefields and Session Resets

Hero Battlefield has Blood Rose, Wings of Dawn, and Guard of Order fields. Matching faction heroes are recommended. Each hero can enter only one battlefield per session, so plan all three teams before committing your strongest heroes.

New sessions start on Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday, resetting challenge progress. Unlocked chapters remain selectable; the reset does not mean you must unlock them from the beginning.

New Chapters, Replays, and Quick Fight

  • Clear your current highest chapter for the first time to unlock the next chapter.
  • Only one additional new chapter can be challenged per event session. Do not plan an unlimited chapter climb in one refresh.
  • You may challenge any unlocked chapter. Replaying a cleared chapter gives you a manageable reward target when the highest chapter is too difficult, but it does not unlock a new chapter.
  • Fully clear Blood Rose Lv. 20 to unlock Quick Fight.

Choose a chapter your assigned team can finish. A published power threshold does not guarantee a win; use your battle result to decide when to attempt the next chapter. For hero investment priorities, use the Heroes Guide.

Give Glory Badges a Job

Stage rewards include Glory Badges, which the Glory Shop exchanges for items such as universal orange hero fragments.

If migration is part of your plan, account for the weekly Migration Pass before spending the same currency elsewhere: the State Migration Guide lists the pass at 5,000 Glory Badges, limited to one per week. Otherwise, buy toward the next hero upgrade you need.
