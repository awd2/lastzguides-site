Last Z Tactical Plugins Guide: Costs, Skills, and Resonance

Vehicle plugins • 0★–8★ costs • Last Z Guides

Quick Answer

Each formation vehicle has four faction-matched Tactical Plugin slots. An orange plugin goes from 0★ to 8★ for 58,486,700 Plugin EXP, 57 exact duplicate copies, and 20 catalysts; the star-up cost curve is the same across faction sets. Each star has eight markers, and Plugin Resonance follows the lowest-starred of the four installed orange plugins.

How Tactical Plugins Work

Plugins belong to a formation's vehicle, with a separate set for each faction. The four slots are Opening, Offensive, Defensive, and Jamming. Equip the set matching that formation's faction; switching the vehicle's faction uninstalls its current plugins.

Stars 1★–5★ appear gold; stars 6★–10★ turn red one at a time. Star-ups use universal Plugin EXP or EXP gained by feeding green, blue, and purple plugins. The 1★ material gate needs 20 catalysts; later gates require duplicates of the exact same orange plugin. Orange plugins cannot be fed to another plugin for EXP; keep them for their own upgrades or exact-copy gates.

Each orange plugin contributes 174,000 power at 0★ and 1,150,000 at 8★. The secondary-stat track shares eight ticks per star: Troop HP runs from 0.4% to 12.4%; Troop ATK and DEF use five times that track (2% to 62%), and Troop DMG uses twice that track (0.8% to 24.8%). Skill effects also rise with stars; the shield factor gains 30 per star.

Star-Up Mechanics: Eight Markers per Star

Every star has eight markers. EXP fills the ordinary markers; diamond markers are material gates. An EXP marker and a gate give the same stat and power increase within that star, so an unfinished star still advances the plugin.

The 1★ final gate takes 20 catalysts. Later gates take exact orange duplicates. The final gate alone needs 1 copy at 3★, 5 at 6★, 8 at 7★, and 10 at 8★; earlier gates within those stars account for the rest of each row's copy cost.

What Is Known Above 8★

The 9★ track uses the same eight-marker layout as 5★–8★. Its first two EXP markers cost 5,777,000 and 5,892,500 EXP (11,669,500 together). Each 9★ marker adds +64 to the primary hero stat and 29,000 power; the secondary increase per marker is +1.5% Troop ATK or DEF, +0.6% Troop DMG, or +0.3% Troop HP.

A growth-rate projection puts the full 9★ EXP cost near 30.7 million and 10★ near 42.3 million, or roughly 89.2 million and 131.5 million cumulative EXP from 0★. These are estimates, not complete upgrade costs: the remaining EXP markers and exact-copy gate bills for 9★–10★ are unknown. Do not treat the projections as a materials budget.

Plugin Chests: Targeted Copies and Orange Odds

An Orange Plugin Choice Box lets you select one of the 12 orange plugins. A random orange result draws from all 12, so a specific plugin has a 1-in-12 share of those results; three factions mean only 1 in 3 random orange results belongs to the faction you need. Chest pity rises after misses and guarantees an orange by the listed open count.

Plugin Resonance: Level the Four Slots Together

Four installed orange plugins activate Plugin Resonance. Its level is the lowest star level among the installed plugins: one 5★ beside three 3★ plugins still gives 3★ resonance. A higher plugin still improves its own stats and skill; raising the lowest slot is what advances the shared bonus.

Reset a Plugin

Uninstalling a plugin is free. Plugin Reset is available after uninstalling and refunds invested star-up EXP, exact duplicates, and catalysts. Green, blue, or purple plugins previously fed for EXP return as the same amount of EXP, rather than as the original plugin items. This makes it possible to change which orange plugin receives your resources without losing the star-up investment.
