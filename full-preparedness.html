Last Z Full Preparedness: Themes, Windows, and Chests

Event Guide • Updated November 12, 2026 • Last Z Guides

Quick Answer

Full Preparedness runs in 4-hour windows, each with one theme: Age of Science, Army Expansion, Hero Progression, Mod Vehicle Boost, or Shelter Upgrade. Spend matching items inside the window to fill up to 3 chests, and save big spends for windows that match the Alliance Duel day — the same action scores in both.

What Scores in Each Theme

Each window rewards one kind of progress. Use the matching items in that window, not before it.

ThemeWhat earns pointsMatching Alliance Duel day
Age of ScienceFinishing research and using research speedupsDay 3 (Wednesday)
Army ExpansionTraining and promoting troopsDay 5 (Friday), Holistic Growth
Hero ProgressionHero upgrades with skill books and hero fragments, plus Prime RecruitmentDay 4 (Thursday)
Mod Vehicle BoostVehicle modification with blueprints, Golden Wrenches and component boxesDay 1 (Monday)
Shelter UpgradeFinishing building upgrades and using construction speedupsDay 2 (Tuesday)

Older guides call Hero Progression "Hero Initiative"; it is the same theme.

4-Hour Windows and Chests

Each window lasts 4 hours and has one theme. The points you earn in that window fill up to 3 chests. A day has six windows, so you can open up to 18 chests.

Hero Progression and Mod Vehicle Boost are the easiest windows to fill from daily play; Shelter Upgrade and Age of Science reward the big timers you saved for.

Score in Alliance Duel at the Same Time

When the window's theme matches the Alliance Duel day, one action scores in both events. Save large spends — research speedups, construction speedups, hero fragments, blueprints — for those overlaps: Mod Vehicle Boost on Monday, Shelter Upgrade on Tuesday, Age of Science on Wednesday, Hero Progression on Thursday, and Army Expansion on Friday.

Ranking and SvS Weeks

You are ranked against players with similar power and activity; your place shows at the bottom of the event screen. In SvS weeks, first place in Full Preparedness adds points toward Invasion Rights, so pushing for it helps your state.

FAQ

How many Full Preparedness chests can I get per day?

Up to 3 in each 4-hour window, so up to 18 a day.

Is Hero Progression the same as Hero Initiative?

Yes. Hero Initiative is the older name of the same Full Preparedness theme.
