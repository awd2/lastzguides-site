Last Z Tactical Master — Badge Costs and Research Tree

Research Costs • September 10, 2026 • Last Z Guides

Quick Answer

Tactical Master costs 6,000,590 badges across 33 nodes and 470 levels. Troop combat bonuses and faction hero ATK/DEF, ending in Crippling Strike and Bulwark.

Does the total include earlier branches?

No. The total covers this branch only. Earlier research, building upgrades, other resources and speedups are excluded.
