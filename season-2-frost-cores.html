Last Z Season 2 Frost Core Guide - Frostbanes, Snow Leopards, and Yetis

Season Guide • Updated September 8, 2026 • Last Z Guides • Checked with server 403 players

Quick Answer

Use Frostbane reward attempts as your default Frost Core routine. Join scheduled Hunter Yeti rallies when they occur; use Snow Leopards for first-clear or event rewards within your resistance range.

  1. 1Frostbanes · 30 reward attempts per day in week one; 15 with doubled rewards afterward
  2. 2Hunter Yeti · 2 rewards daily Monday–Saturday; 5 during Sunday’s event; no Virus Resistance requirement
  3. 3Spending · reserve Frost Cores for the Bio Lab checkpoint your Mastery path needs

Daily Frostbane Attempts and Fuel Costs

Frostbanes are the Winter counterpart of the Season 1 Boomer-style target.

The first week sets your pace for the season. Prioritize your daily Frostbane rewards early: the Frost Cores help you unlock and upgrade seasonal buildings sooner. Completing 30 normal reward attempts and 15 double-reward attempts gives the same daily total at the same target level. Higher-level targets change the payout; the reason to prioritize week one is earlier building progress.

Yetis Need Alliance Coordination

Hunter Yeti gives 2 reward attempts per day Monday–Saturday and 5 during Sunday’s event. Virus Resistance is not required. Join an alliance rally with enough strength for the target while you still have reward attempts.

Rewards can include Ice Blooms, Frost Cores, Snowballs, Modification Blueprints, and resources. Follow the rally leader’s target level and timing rather than trying a stronger Yeti because your Bio Lab level increased.

Where to Spend Frost Cores

Reserve Frost Cores for Bio Lab 26 to unlock level 60 Frostbanes before Combat Mastery. To keep that access when switching, reach Bio Lab 28 first. If Ice Blooms are the missing resource, Smart Greenhouse production addresses that shortage; more Frost Cores alone will not unlock the upgrade.

After reaching your checkpoint, compare Command Post and the cheaper Icy Arsenal or Icy Fort levels. The Bio Lab guide covers that spending decision and the exception for imminent alliance PvP.

Spend seasonal resources before Winter ends: unused Frost Cores and Ice Blooms expire.
