Quick Answer

Best Last Z gear upgrade order: equip your main five with orange gear, enhance all 20 pieces to level 20, take the first promotion step on every piece, then complete each hexagon evenly. Stop before hexagon 5 unless you are a heavy spender; F2P and light spenders can use earned Forging Stones; skip paid red mythic transformation.

  1. ① Orange first · where to get orange equipment
  2. ② Upgrade evenly · all to 20 → first promotion step on all
  3. ③ First promotion = best value · 100 cores + 1M Zent
  4. ④ Merit Shop weekly · orange box until all heroes geared
  5. ⑤ Skip 5th hexagon · uses 12,800 cores — 50% of the five-hexagon total

💡 Full cost per piece: 68M Zent + 25,400 Power Cores + 10 orange items for 5 hexagons.

How to Enhance Gear

Enhancement (Alloy)

Increases gear level using Enhancement Alloy. Access via Heroes → Equipment → Enhance.

Level caps by gear state:

  • Base orange gear: Level 50 max
  • Each completed hexagon: +5 levels (55, 60, 65, 70, 75)
  • Mythic transformation: +25 levels (max 100)

Important: You must reach level 20 before you can unlock promotion. Always enhance to level 20 first, then start promoting.

Promotion (Hexagons)

Orange gear has 5 hexagons, each with 6 steps = 30 total promotion steps.

Promotion uses:

  • Power Cores — all steps
  • Zent (money) — all steps
  • Orange equipment — hexagons 4 and 5 only

First promotion step is HUGE: Only costs 100 Power Cores + 1M Zent but gives massive stats. Prioritize the first promotion step on every piece; a completed star requires all six steps.

Optimal Upgrade Strategy

  1. Merit Shop: Buy orange equipment choice box EVERY week until all 5 heroes have full orange (20 pieces total)
  2. Enhance to 20: Level up all orange gear to level 20 (unlocks promotion)
  3. First hexagon first step: Take the first promotion step on every piece; this is not a completed star
  4. Complete hexagon 1: Finish all 6 steps of hexagon 1 on ALL gear
  5. Continue evenly: Hexagon 2 on all gear, then hexagon 3, then hexagon 4
  6. Stop at hexagon 4: Hexagon 5 costs nearly HALF of total resources — skip unless whale

DON'T: Rush one piece to hexagon 5 while others are at hexagon 1. Power per resource is much better when upgrading evenly across all pieces.

First Step Efficiency

The very first promotion step (hexagon 1, step 1) costs only:

  • 100 Power Cores
  • 1M Zent

This gives MASSIVE stat boost for minimal cost. Always get all gear to this point first.

Hexagon 5 Warning

Hexagon 5 alone costs:

  • 12,800 Power Cores (50% of total cores)
  • 18.8M Zent (28% of total)
  • 6 orange items (60% of total items)

Unless you're a whale, stop at hexagon 4. The power gain per resource drops dramatically at hexagon 5.

Exclusive Equipment

Exclusive equipment is tied to a specific hero. Check that hero’s unlock requirement and equipment effect before comparing it with a replacement hero.

Unlocking and upgrading are different costs

  • Unlock: identify the hero-specific item or fragments required to obtain the equipment.
  • Upgrade: check which materials improve equipment you already own.
  • Availability: read the current event or shop offer. Owning generic upgrade fragments does not mean you can unlock a hero’s equipment for free.

Do not assume every exclusive item becomes free at the next season start. Compare the acquisition cost, upgrade cost, and effect on a hero you actually plan to use.

Mythic forging: requirements and costs

Orange equipment has five Forging Stone stages (+1 to +5) before MAX transforms it into red equipment. You can use the stone upgrades without paying for the final transformation.

Enhancement levels, promotion stars and forging stages are separate. A completed promotion star means all six steps of one hexagon, upgraded with Power Cores. Meet both the level and star requirement for each forging stage.

The five stone stages cost 680 Forging Stones and 260M Electricity per piece in total. MAX adds 300 stones, 50 Pulse Modules and 200M Electricity: 980 stones, 50 modules and 460M Electricity for the full path.

Where to get Pulse Modules

Pulse Modules are needed for the MAX transformation to red equipment; the +1 to +5 stages use none. The confirmed sources on our checked server are expensive event milestones and special packs. Do not budget for a free Alliance Duel supply.

For F2P and light spenders, use Forging Stones you earn to improve eligible orange gear without committing to red transformation.

Red transformation adds 25 enhancement levels to the existing cap; level 100 requires all five completed promotion stars. Reaching MAX forging does not replace Power Core promotion.

Use Forging Stones and Pulse Modules on Tuesday, Shelter Upgrade, for Alliance Duel points.

Common Gear Mistakes

  • Delaying orange gear upgrades — Prioritize the orange pieces on your main five heroes. Purple caps at 30 and blue at 20; neither supports promotion.
  • Spreading gear across many heroes — Focus on main 5 only (20 pieces)
  • Rushing hexagon 5 — Costs 50% of total Power Cores for minimal gain
  • Skipping first promotion step — 100 cores + 1M zent gives massive value
  • Not upgrading evenly — Don't take one piece to hexagon 3 while others at 0
  • Missing Merit Shop — Buy orange choice box EVERY week
  • Hoarding spare low-rarity gear — Use it to enhance your orange equipment
  • Paying for red transformation too early — improve eligible orange gear with earned Forging Stones; buy Pulse Modules only with a planned spending budget.

FAQ

What is the best / mythic equipment in Last Z?

Orange (legendary) gear is the best. It can be promoted through 5 hexagons and enhanced to level 75 (or 100 with mythic). For DPS heroes, prioritize Attack %. For tanks, prioritize HP %. Focus on your main 5 heroes only.

Should I upgrade purple gear?

Prioritize orange gear. Purple gear caps at level 30 and cannot be promoted. If you already enhanced a purple piece, use it to enhance orange gear: all Enhancement Alloy invested in the purple piece carries over.

How many hexagons should I complete?

For F2P: Stop at 4 hexagons. Hexagon 5 costs 12,800 Power Cores (50% of total) and 6 orange items (60% of total). The efficiency drops dramatically. Only whales should complete hexagon 5.

How do I use Power Cores in Last Z gear?

Enhance to level 20 first — this unlocks promotion. Then do the first promotion step on all gear (100 cores + 1M zent each). The first step gives massive stats for minimal cost.

How much does full gear promotion cost?

Per piece: 25,400 Power Cores + 68M Zent + 10 orange items for all 5 hexagons. Total for 20 pieces (5 heroes): 508,000 Power Cores + 1.36B Zent + 200 orange items.

What is Mythic equipment?

Mythic forging improves orange gear through +1 to +5 with Forging Stones. MAX transforms it into red gear and adds 25 enhancement levels. The five stone stages can be used without buying the Pulse Modules needed for MAX.

How do I unlock mythic transformation?

MAX requires forging +5, equipment level 60 and three completed Power Core promotion stars. The final step costs 300 Forging Stones, 50 Pulse Modules and 200M Electricity.
