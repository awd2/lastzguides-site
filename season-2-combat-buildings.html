Last Z Season 2 Combat Buildings Guide - Command Post, Icy Arsenal, and Icy Fort

Season Guide • Updated August 1, 2026 • 6 min read • Last Z Guides • Checked with server 403 players

Quick Answer

Once the resistance engine is ready, use Command Post as your first combat-building priority. If its next level costs disproportionately more Frost Cores and Ice Blooms, take cheaper Icy Arsenal or Icy Fort levels and then return to Command Post; there is no fixed stop level.

  1. 1Bio Lab checkpoint·level 26 gives level 60 Frostbane access before the Combat switch; reach 28 to keep that access after switching
  2. 2Combat stats·Command Post gives Season Troop DMG, Icy Arsenal gives Troop ATK, and Icy Fort gives Troop DEF in every combat situation
  3. 3Greenhouse handoff·push the 4 base Smart Greenhouses toward 25-26; a fifth requires Wartime Investment

What Season 2 Combat Buildings Do

Command Post, Icy Arsenal, and Icy Fort are personal Season 2 Winter buildings. Their job is combat pressure, not resource income or resistance tempo.

That distinction matters. Bio Lab and Smart Greenhouse help unlock the season engine. Combat buildings help you fight better once that engine is already moving.

Building Main value Best use
Command Post Season Troop DMG Multiplies seasonal troop damage in both attack and defense
Icy Arsenal Season Troop ATK Increases troop ATK in both attack and defense
Icy Fort Season Troop DEF Increases troop DEF in both attack and defense

If your account is still stuck on early resistance progress, combat buildings can look powerful while still being the wrong first spend.

Command Post, Icy Arsenal, and Icy Fort Values

Use these values to understand the ceiling before you commit builder time and seasonal resources.

Building Base F2P cap Per-level value Value at base cap
Command Post 30 +1% Season Troop DMG +30% Season Troop DMG
Icy Arsenal 50 +3% Season Troop ATK +150% Season Troop ATK
Icy Fort 50 +3% Season Troop DEF +150% Season Troop DEF

These are the base F2P caps; Season Pass or Wartime Investment can add levels. All three combat buildings require Frost Cores and Ice Blooms, but their resource ratios differ. Command Post remains the first priority; however, when its next level costs disproportionately more than available Icy Arsenal or Icy Fort levels, take the cheaper upgrades first and then return to Command Post.

Bio Lab 26 vs 28: The Combat Switch Checkpoint

Bio Lab 26 and Bio Lab 28 solve different problems. Bio Lab 26 gives you the resistance needed for maximum level 60 Frostbane access while you remain outside the Combat Mastery path. If you switch into Combat, reach Bio Lab 28 first to keep killing level 60 Frostbanes after losing the Support-side resistance.

Do not push Bio Lab 28 as if level 26 has no value. Level 26 completes the Frostbane access checkpoint before the switch; level 28 protects that access when you move into Combat.

Goal Recommended checkpoint Why it matters
Kill level 60 Frostbanes before switching into Combat Bio Lab level 26 Provides the maximum Frostbane access checkpoint while you remain outside Combat
Switch into Combat and keep killing level 60 Frostbanes Bio Lab level 28 Compensates for the resistance lost when switching away from the Support path
Begin heavier combat-building investment After the Bio Lab checkpoint required by your current Mastery path Prevents combat upgrades from breaking your Frostbane farming access

Use Bio Lab 26 as the pre-Combat Frostbane checkpoint and Bio Lab 28 as the Combat-switch checkpoint. That distinction lets you start combat-building progress without overstating how early level 60 Frostbanes become available.

Smart Greenhouse Level 25-26 Is Usually Enough

Smart Greenhouse is important early because it feeds Ice Bloom income. But it is not usually the building you keep pushing forever.

Every player can build 4 Smart Greenhouses. Wartime Investment unlocks a fifth. Smart Greenhouses usually stop being worth heavy investment around level 25-26; once their Ice Bloom income supports the Bio Lab path, shift more Frost Cores and Ice Blooms toward Command Post, Icy Arsenal, and Icy Fort.

Smart Greenhouse level Planning meaning Best next action
Below 25 Ice Bloom income may still be a real bottleneck Keep improving Greenhouse income if Bio Lab is waiting on Ice Blooms
25-26 Usually enough Ice Bloom income for the core Season 2 engine Start shifting more attention into combat buildings
Above 26 Often lower practical value than combat-building progress Only push higher if your own Bio Lab path is still clearly Ice Bloom blocked

The mistake is overbuilding Smart Greenhouses after they have already solved the Ice Bloom problem. Once the income is good enough, combat buildings usually give the next clearer account value.

Combat Building Upgrade Priority

For most players, the first priority is not maxing one combat building immediately. The first priority is making sure combat upgrades do not break the Season 2 engine.

  1. Reach the correct Bio Lab checkpoint. Use Bio Lab 26 for level 60 Frostbane access before Combat, or Bio Lab 28 before switching into Combat and keeping that access.
  2. Stabilize Ice Bloom income. Level your 4 base Smart Greenhouses toward 25-26; Wartime Investment adds a fifth Greenhouse.
  3. Start with Command Post. Its Season Troop DMG makes it the first combat-building priority.
  4. Compare the next resource costs. All combat buildings use Frost Cores and Ice Blooms in different ratios.
  5. Take efficient Arsenal and Fort levels. If the next Command Post level is disproportionately expensive, upgrade cheaper Icy Arsenal and Icy Fort levels immediately instead of forcing Command Post alone.
  6. Return to Command Post. Keep it as the primary path while using lower-cost ATK and DEF levels to avoid wasting seasonal resources.

There is no fixed stop level for the three combat buildings. Recheck the Frost Core and Ice Bloom cost of each available level and buy the strongest practical combination rather than keeping the buildings equal.

When Bio Lab Still Comes First

Bio Lab should still beat combat buildings when it is the thing blocking better seasonal access. A weak resistance engine can make every other Season 2 system feel slower.

Situation Best move Why
Bio Lab is below level 26 and level 60 Frostbanes are the goal Reach Bio Lab 26 first Level 26 unlocks maximum Frostbane access before the Combat switch
Bio Lab is blocked by Ice Blooms Improve Smart Greenhouse income up to about 25-26 Weak Ice Bloom flow stalls the engine, but pushing Greenhouses too far can delay combat value
Bio Lab is blocked by Frost Cores Protect Frostbane and alliance target value Spending Frost Cores elsewhere can delay the next resistance step
No urgent PvP window Stay progression-first Combat stats are less valuable if the alliance is not using them right now

This is the main F2P and low-spend rule: do not buy a better fight today by making the rest of the season slower.

When To Push Command Post, Icy Arsenal, and Icy Fort

Combat buildings become more attractive when your alliance is about to use the stats. That usually means city fights, Glory War, coordinated rallies, or another Season 2 PvP window.

For the event-specific coordination layer, see the Glory War guide before committing start times, defensive building placement, cross-server support, or same-server point-farming rules.

Start pushing them harder when these are true:

  • Your Bio Lab matches your Mastery plan. Level 26 supports level 60 Frostbanes before Combat; level 28 keeps that access after switching.
  • Smart Greenhouses are around level 25-26. Ice Bloom income should usually be good enough by then.
  • Your alliance has a real fight coming. Combat buildings are better when the stats are used immediately.
  • The next levels are resource-efficient. Compare the Frost Core and Ice Bloom cost of Command Post, Icy Arsenal, and Icy Fort before choosing.

If those conditions are not true, keep combat buildings moving slowly while the resistance engine stays first.

Command Post vs Icy Arsenal vs Icy Fort

Command Post comes first, but it should not consume every Frost Core and Ice Bloom when cheaper combat levels are available elsewhere.

Current choice Best move Decision rule
Starting combat buildings Command Post Season Troop DMG makes it the first overall combat priority
Next Command Post level is still affordable Continue Command Post Keep the primary path moving while its resource cost remains reasonable
Next Command Post level is disproportionately expensive Upgrade Icy Arsenal and Icy Fort Cheaper +3% Troop ATK and +3% Troop DEF levels can increase troop strength more efficiently
Combat building costs are close again Return to Command Post first Do not permanently abandon the main Season Troop DMG path

Icy Arsenal and Icy Fort are not attack-only and defense-only choices. Troop ATK and Troop DEF apply in both directions; choose between their available levels by resource cost, not by whether you expect to attack or defend next.

What Happens When Season 2 Winter Ends?

Season 2 combat buildings are temporary. When Winter ends, Command Post, Icy Arsenal, Icy Fort, Smart Greenhouses, and the other seasonal buildings disappear, and their building buffs disappear with them.

  • Ice Blooms expire: they do not carry into the next season.
  • Frost Cores expire: do not save them for a future season.
  • Seasonal building buffs disappear: Troop DMG, ATK, and DEF from these buildings are temporary.
  • Mastery progress remains: this is the seasonal progression that carries forward.

Before the season closes, use remaining Ice Blooms and Frost Cores for the current Winter objective. Do not treat them as a reserve for the next season.

Common Combat Building Mistakes

  • Rushing Icy Arsenal while Bio Lab is stalled: more Troop ATK does not fix weak seasonal access.
  • Ignoring Icy Fort completely: damage is attractive, but city and rally fights also punish weak defense.
  • Upgrading combat buildings evenly with no plan: equal levels are not the goal; useful timing is the goal.
  • Ignoring the next upgrade cost: forcing an expensive Command Post level can waste the chance to buy several cheaper Arsenal or Fort levels.
  • Switching into Combat too early: Bio Lab 26 gives level 60 Frostbane access before the switch, but Bio Lab 28 is required to keep that access after switching.
  • Overbuilding Smart Greenhouses: after level 25-26, they usually stop being the best heavy investment because Ice Bloom income should be enough.
  • Spending before checking the next Bio Lab requirement: this is still the easiest way to slow Season 2 progress.

Daily Combat Building Checklist

  1. Check Bio Lab first. Use level 26 for level 60 Frostbanes before Combat, or level 28 before switching and keeping that access.
  2. Check Smart Greenhouse income. If Greenhouses are below 25-26 and Ice Blooms are the problem, fix income first.
  3. Check Frost Core pressure. If Bio Lab needs cores soon, do not spend them casually.
  4. Check alliance calendar. Push combat harder before real PvP windows, not randomly.
  5. Compare all three next-level costs. Command Post gives Troop DMG, Icy Arsenal gives Troop ATK, and Icy Fort gives Troop DEF.
  6. Use Command Post as the baseline combat building. It gives Season Troop DMG and has a base F2P cap of level 30.

Season 2 Combat Buildings FAQ

Should I upgrade Command Post, Icy Arsenal, or Icy Fort first?

Start with Command Post for Season Troop DMG. If its next level becomes disproportionately expensive, take cheaper Icy Arsenal levels for Season Troop ATK and Icy Fort levels for Season Troop DEF, then return to Command Post.

What Bio Lab level do I need before switching into Combat for level 60 Frostbanes?

Bio Lab 26 gives level 60 Frostbane access before the Combat switch. Reach Bio Lab 28 before switching into Combat if you want to keep killing level 60 Frostbanes afterward.

How high should I upgrade Smart Greenhouses?

Every player can build 4 Smart Greenhouses, and Wartime Investment unlocks a fifth. Heavy pushing usually stops around level 25-26 once Ice Bloom income supports the Bio Lab path.

What does Command Post do?

Command Post gives +1% Season Troop DMG per level. Its base F2P cap is level 30; Season Pass or Wartime Investment can add levels. Compare its next cost with cheaper Arsenal and Fort levels instead of forcing Command Post alone.

What does Icy Arsenal do?

Icy Arsenal adds +3% Season Troop ATK per level in every fight. F2P progression reaches level 50, with extra levels available through Season Pass or Wartime Investment.

What does Icy Fort do?

Icy Fort strengthens Troop DEF by +3% per level whether you attack or defend. Its base F2P endpoint is level 50; paid season systems can extend the building.

Should F2P players rush Season 2 combat buildings?

Usually no. Reach Bio Lab 26 for level 60 Frostbane access before Combat, or Bio Lab 28 before switching into Combat and keeping that access. Stabilize Smart Greenhouses around level 25-26, then start with Command Post and compare the Frost Core and Ice Bloom cost of every next combat level.

Before Pushing Combat Buildings

  • Before spending hard into combat buildings, check Bio Lab level, Smart Greenhouse income, Frost Core pressure, and your alliance PvP calendar.
  • If your alliance needs combat stats for an active fight, follow that call over a passive farming plan.
