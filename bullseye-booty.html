Last Z Bullseye Booty Guide — When to Stop

Event Strategy • Updated August 2, 2026 • Last Z Guides

Quick Answer

Save about 140–150 Bullseye Bullets before you commit to a Bullseye Booty run. Complete every event task that gives free Bullseye Bullets, then spend diamonds only if you need more bullets to finish Round 12. Claim the Orange Equipment Choice Box and stop.

About 140–150 bullets is a planning reserve, not a guarantee. The cost of reaching Round 12 varies between runs.

Progress resets when the event ends, but unused Bullseye Bullets carry over. Save every bullet left after the Round 12 chest for the next event.

The Best Bullseye Booty Plan

1. Complete Every Free-Bullet Task

Start with the event task list. Finish every task that awards Bullseye Bullets before deciding whether to spend diamonds. Free bullets reduce the amount you may need to buy and make the Round 12 push safer for F2P players.

2. Build a Round 12 Reserve

Use about 140–150 Bullseye Bullets as your planning target. This is a buffer based on completed runs, not a guaranteed price. Bullseye Booty is random enough that two players can spend different numbers of bullets to reach the same round.

If your total is well below that range, saving for the next event is safer than starting a run that may end before the Orange Equipment Choice Box.

3. Use Diamonds Only to Close the Gap

Do not buy Bullseye Bullets before collecting the free event bullets. If you are close to Round 12 and need more shots, diamonds can be worth spending to finish the milestone. The goal is the Orange Equipment Choice Box, not spending the full diamond allowance automatically.

4. Stop After Round 12

Round 12 awards the Orange Equipment Choice Box. For F2P and low-spend players, this is the repeatable stopping point: claim the box, stop shooting, and carry the remaining Bullseye Bullets into the next event.

What Carries Over Between Bullseye Booty Events?

Unused Bullseye Bullets carry over to the next Bullseye Booty event. Round progress does not.

That makes leftover bullets valuable. Stopping after Round 12 is not wasting them — it is building the reserve for the next Orange Equipment Choice Box.

Does Bullseye Booty Have Pity?

Bullseye Booty has no pity. Do not treat a previous expensive round as proof that the next one will be cheaper, and do not assume a fixed number of bullets will always reach Round 12.

The practical protection is preparation: collect every free bullet, keep a reserve, and use diamonds only when they can complete the Round 12 push.

Bullseye Booty Mistakes to Avoid

  • Buying bullets before completing the free-bullet tasks.
  • Starting a serious run without enough reserve to make Round 12 realistic.
  • Treating 140–150 bullets as a guaranteed cost.
  • Continuing after the Orange Equipment Choice Box without a separate high-spend goal.
  • Spending leftover bullets because progress resets; the bullets themselves carry over.
  • Applying Lucky Discounter or Lucky Choice Chest rules to Bullseye Booty. They are separate events.

FAQ

How many Bullseye Bullets should I save?

About 140–150 is a practical planning reserve for a Round 12 attempt, but it is not a guarantee. The actual cost varies between runs.

What is the Round 12 Bullseye Booty reward?

Round 12 awards the Orange Equipment Choice Box.

Do Bullseye Bullets carry over?

Yes. Unused Bullseye Bullets carry over to the next Bullseye Booty event, but your round progress resets.

Does Bullseye Booty have pity?

No. Bullseye Booty has no pity, so an expensive run does not guarantee cheaper results later.

Should F2P players spend diamonds on Bullseye Bullets?

Complete every free-bullet task first. Spend diamonds only when extra bullets can help you finish Round 12 and claim the Orange Equipment Choice Box.

Are Bullseye Booty and Lucky Discounter the same event?

No. Bullseye Booty, Lucky Discounter, and Lucky Choice Chest are separate events with different currencies and rules.
