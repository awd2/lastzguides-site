Last Z Season 2 Bio Lab Guide - Smart Greenhouse, Ice Blooms, and Resistance

Season Guide • Updated August 1, 2026 • Last Z Guides

Quick Answer

Bio Lab 26 gives access to level 60 Frostbanes before the Combat Mastery switch. To keep that access when switching, reach Bio Lab 28 first. Prioritize the Bio Lab level your Mastery path needs before optional combat upgrades.

  1. 1Ice Blooms missing · Smart Greenhouses produce them; you can build 4, with a fifth from Wartime Investment
  2. 2Frost Cores missing · farm rewarded Frostbanes and join scheduled alliance targets
  3. 3Hunter Yeti · does not require Virus Resistance; do not delay its rallies to raise Bio Lab

What Bio Lab Does

Bio Lab supplies Virus Resistance for Frostbanes and Snow Leopards. Smart Greenhouse supplies Ice Blooms, not resistance. Bio Lab upgrades consume both Ice Blooms and Frost Cores, with Ice Bloom requirements several times larger.

Hunter Yeti is the exception: it has no Virus Resistance requirement. Choose those rallies by alliance timing, rally strength, and remaining reward attempts; see the Frost Core farming guide.

When To Shift Into Command Post, Icy Arsenal, and Icy Fort

Start with Command Post after the Bio Lab checkpoint for your Mastery path. If its next level costs disproportionately more Frost Cores or Ice Blooms, take cheaper Icy Arsenal or Icy Fort levels instead of raising all three evenly.

An imminent alliance city fight or Glory War can justify combat buildings earlier. The tradeoff is delaying the Bio Lab level that opens your next Frostbane target. Switching to the Combat Mastery tree is a separate decision: reach Bio Lab 28 first if you want to retain level 60 Frostbane access. See Mastery Hall for the tree and reset rules.
