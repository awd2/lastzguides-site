Last Z Best Formations: Faction Teams, 3+2 and Hero Placement

Formation Guide • Updated September 7, 2026 • Season 4 • Last Z Guides

Quick Answer

Build around the combat heroes and seasonal pairs you have developed. Compare a five-hero faction team with a 3+2 option only after checking which talents each lineup activates. Neither pattern wins every matchup.

  • Seasonal pairs: Bella + Licia, Alma + Liliana, or Harleyena + Dodemeki; check the S3 hero’s talent and star requirement.
  • Faction counters: Wings of Dawn counters Blood Rose; Guard of Order counters Wings of Dawn; Blood Rose counters Guard of Order. A counter advantage does not guarantee a win.
  • Placement: start with two defense heroes in front and three damage heroes behind, then adjust for your actual roster.
  • Comparison: keep mode, troop tier, equipment, and temporary buffs consistent; compare battle results as well as power.

Faction Counter Triangle

Understanding counters is the foundation of formation building:

  • Wings of Dawn → counters → Blood Rose
  • Guard of Order → counters → Wings of Dawn
  • Blood Rose → counters → Guard of Order

Wings of Dawn has a counter advantage against Blood Rose. That makes your opponents’ lineups relevant, but it does not establish that Wings of Dawn is the best investment for every account. Compare the cost of changing faction with improving your existing team.

Best Formation Builds

Five heroes of one faction

Use this as a comparison point when you already have a developed faction team. Five matching heroes can activate formation bonuses and hero talents that require a full faction lineup. Check both before removing a hero; these benefits are not limited to displayed power or to paying players.

A 3+2 team with seasonal pairs

A mixed team is worth testing when it preserves a useful pair or adds a developed hero whose contribution outweighs lost faction bonuses. Three heroes plus two heroes does not automatically activate a seasonal synergy.

For Wings of Dawn and Blood Rose combinations, check Alma + Liliana and Bella + Licia. The S3 partner’s talent and five-star extension matter when your plan depends on applying its benefit across troop types. Compare the mixed lineup with your developed faction team before investing in the change.

Choosing the fifth hero

Compare the available hero’s stars, active skills, equipment, troop match, and effect on existing pair and faction talents. A faction label alone does not make an underdeveloped hero the better choice.

Hero Selection Priority

Choose a hero for the effects they add to the actual formation:

  1. Keep the named partners needed for your seasonal talents.
  2. Check whether each bonus affects the hero, matching troops, the faction, or all troop types.
  3. Check deployment separately: “all troops” does not by itself mean the hero can stay outside the lineup.
  4. Keep account-development skills separate from combat-slot selection. A useful construction or research skill does not require that hero to occupy a combat slot.

For hero-by-hero investment priorities, see the Heroes Guide.

Common Mistakes

  • Breaking a seasonal pair without checking the lost talent.
  • Treating a faction counter as a guaranteed win.
  • Switching to an underdeveloped hero solely to complete a 3+2 pattern.
  • Counting a troop-type bonus that does not affect the troops you send.
  • Comparing power or battle results across different buffs, equipment, or troop tiers.

How to Build Your Formation

  1. Start with your developed combat heroes and matching troops.
  2. Identify active pair talents and five-faction requirements.
  3. Check the role and position of each hero.
  4. Change one lineup choice at a time where practical.
  5. Compare results in the mode you want to improve.

For power optimization, see the Formation Power Guide.

Troop Types Explained

Last Z has three troop types, each aligned with a faction:

Troop Type Best Faction Strengths Top Heroes (Buffers)
Assaulters Blood Rose High damage, frontline pressure Selena, Yu Chan, Licia
Shooters Wings of Dawn Ranged DPS, backline safety Queenie, Liliana, Alma (+Shooter buffs)
Riders Guard of Order Mobility, flanking Amber, Dodemeki, Sakura (+Rider buffs)

Matching Heroes to Troops

  • Read the troop type named by each skill or talent.
  • Check the current star effect; do not count a five-star extension before unlocking it.
  • Check deployment requirements independently of troop scope.
  • Use troops that receive the bonuses your lineup actually activates.

Which Troop Type to Focus?

Focus on ONE troop type that matches your faction:

  • Blood Rose players → Train Assaulters first, research Assaulter tech
  • Wings of Dawn players → Train Shooters first, research Shooter tech
  • Guard of Order players → Train Riders first, research Rider tech

Use troops that receive the bonuses your lineup actually activates.

FAQ

Best hero combinations: mono-faction or mixed?

Neither is always better. Five matching heroes can activate faction-specific talents; a 3+2 lineup can preserve seasonal pairs or add a stronger developed hero. Compare the active bonuses, troop match, and battle results for your account.

What faction should I focus on?

Wings of Dawn has a counter advantage against Blood Rose. That makes your opponents’ lineups relevant, but it does not establish that Wings of Dawn is the best investment for every account. Compare the cost of changing faction with improving your existing team.

Does hero placement matter?

Start with two defense heroes in front and three damage heroes behind, then adjust for your actual roster.

Should I switch formations for different modes?

For PvP modes, keep your counter formation. For PvE (Zombie Siege), use your highest DPS. For Furylord, switch to match the daily faction rotation.
