How to Activate the Lucky Rose Buff

The Lucky Rose buff is personal. It applies to the sender, not the recipient or the rest of the alliance.

When the Lucky Rose Number Resets

The weekly number resets every Monday at 00:00 Apocalypse Time. After the reset, do not reuse the previous week's number. Wait until the new number and buff appear at the top of this page.

Lucky Rose Buff vs Weekly Gift Rewards

The Lucky Rose buff and the weekly Gift Level reward are separate.

The Lucky Rose requires the exact active number in one gift. The weekly Gift Level reward counts roses sent across the week: sending 10 roses reaches 100 points and awards 6 Orange Hero Fragments plus 3 Purple EXP Chests.

Why Didn't the Buff Activate?

Check these four things:

  • You used this week's number, not last week's.
  • You sent Yellow Roses.
  • You sent the exact number in one gift.
  • You checked the sender's active buffs, not the recipient's account.

If the in-game Lucky Rose screen differs from this page, follow the in-game value and report the mismatch.

Common Questions

Who receives the Lucky Rose buff?

The player who sends the correct number of Yellow Roses receives the personal buff.

Does the recipient receive the buff?

No. Receiving the roses does not activate the Lucky Rose buff.

Does the whole alliance receive the buff?

No. The Lucky Rose buff is personal to the sender.

Can I use last week's number after Monday reset?

No. The previous number expires at Monday's 00:00 Apocalypse Time reset.
