Quick Answer

Best Last Z Alliance Shop priority: keep enough Personal Contribution for a shield or teleport you will actually need, then spend by account stage. New accounts get more from useful hero fragments and Prime Recruitment Tickets; mid-game accounts should favor Badges, Modification Blueprints, and Module Chests; late-game accounts can turn the 2,000-credit 1-hour Speedup Choice Box into training, research, building, or healing speedups.

How the Redesigned Alliance Shop Works

Members spend Personal Contribution on purchases. The alliance holds a separate balance of Alliance Points, and only R4 and R5 can spend those points to restock the shared shop inventory.

  • Shared stock: an item cannot be purchased when the alliance inventory reaches zero.
  • Personal weekly limit: most items also have a per-player limit that resets every Monday at 00:00.
  • Two separate blocks: an item can remain in shared stock but show Sold Out This Week after you use your own limit.
  • Favorites: select the star on an item to receive a notification when an out-of-stock item is restocked.
  • Logs: purchase and restock records help members and officers see what is moving through the shop.

What to Buy at Each Account Stage

New Account: Roster Progress Without Wasting Fragments

Use Prime Recruitment Tickets on a useful hero-scoring window unless your roster is blocking progress now. Katrina and Miranda both belong to Blood Rose, but buy their fragments only while the hero still has a place in your active formations. Do not build every shop hero simply because fragments are available.

Mid Game: Badges, Blueprints, and Modules

Once hero replacements slow down, shift toward resources that support permanent systems. Badges relieve research pressure, Modification Blueprints advance vehicle upgrades, and Lv.1 Random Module Chests support module development. Choose the material blocking your next real upgrade instead of clearing every weekly limit.

Late Game and War: Choice Boxes and Required Movement

The 1-hour Speedup Choice Box becomes stronger when you can select training speedups for sustained fighting, research or building speedups for a planned milestone, or healing speedups for an immediate recovery need. Keep teleports for a known movement problem; a cheap unused teleport is still wasted Personal Contribution.

The Hidden Value: 2,000 Credits per Hour

The old 8-hour speedup offer cost 64,000 credits, or 8,000 credits per hour. The redesigned 1-hour Speedup Choice Box costs 2,000 credits and lets you choose building, research, training, or healing speedups. That is four times as much speedup time for the same Personal Contribution as the old best rate.

  • Current weekly limit: 80 boxes, not unlimited.
  • Maximum weekly spend: 160,000 Personal Contribution for 80 hours.
  • Best timing: select the speedup that matches the active bottleneck or Alliance Duel day.
  • Do not buy blindly: leave enough contribution for an upcoming shield or required teleport.

Top Contribution Doubles Two Everyday Sources

The Support Mastery perk Top Contribution gives +100% Member Points from donating to Alliance Tech and helping allies. If the perk is available to your account, it is a direct way to build Personal Contribution faster through actions you already perform.

Free Alliance Tech donation attempts regenerate every 18 minutes and stack to 20, so a full stack takes six hours. Recommended technology adds 20% to the normal donation reward. Use the free attempts before they sit at the cap.

Diamond donations are optional, not a default daily expense. The first ten cost 110 diamonds when the price starts at two and rises by two each attempt. In the redesign walkthrough, those ten donations returned about 4,000 Personal Contribution with Top Contribution and the shown donation bonuses—enough for two Choice Boxes, or roughly 55 diamonds per hour of speedup. Treat that return as an example rather than a fixed reward, and stop when the rising donation price would cut into your diamond reserve.

8-Hour vs 24-Hour Peace Shield

Three 8-hour Peace Shields cost 18,000 credits for 24 hours of total coverage. One 24-hour shield costs 20,000. The 8-hour route is 2,000 credits cheaper, but only use it when you can renew protection without leaving a gap.

  • Choose 8 hours: for a defined raid window or when you will be online to manage the next shield.
  • Choose 24 hours: when uninterrupted offline coverage matters more than the 2,000-credit difference.
  • Preserve diamonds: buy a stocked Alliance Shop shield before paying diamonds for the same emergency protection.

Why the Shop Runs Out of Stock

Personal Contribution and Alliance Points grow on different sides of the economy. Members can earn contribution from donations, alliance help, rankings, Alliance Duel, trucks, and events, while R4/R5 need the alliance's shared points to keep popular items stocked. An active alliance can therefore have members ready to buy while the shared inventory is empty.

Practical Restock Rules for R4 and R5

  • Keep a shared reserve instead of spending all Alliance Points on one restock.
  • Use purchase records to identify items the alliance actually consumes.
  • Protect stock for shields, required teleports, and broadly useful progression items before niche purchases.
  • Ask members to Favorite out-of-stock priorities so the restock notification reaches the players waiting for them.
  • Do not promise constant Choice Box supply: one member can buy up to 80 per week, so heavy demand can drain the shared stock quickly.

Alliance Shop FAQ

Why does an item say Sold Out This Week when stock remains?

You have reached your personal weekly purchase limit. Shared alliance stock and personal limits are separate; the item can remain available to other members.

When do Alliance Shop purchase limits reset?

The current shop rules say every Monday at 00:00. Use the in-game countdown to confirm the remaining time for your server.

Is the 1-hour Speedup Choice Box unlimited?

No. The current shop shows a weekly personal limit of 80 boxes. Earlier rollout coverage described it as unlimited, but the live September configuration is capped.

Should I buy Peace Shields here or with diamonds?

Use Personal Contribution for a stocked 8-hour or 24-hour Alliance Shop shield first. Keep diamonds as the fallback when stock is empty or protection is needed immediately.
