How LDShop Top-Up Works

LDShop is a separate checkout for Last Z purchases. The order starts on LDShop, and the top-up method you choose connects it to your Last Z account.

Choose the product

Pick the Gold Bar amount or pack. LDShop shows live offers, coupons, and discounted bundle prices on the product page.

Pay on LDShop

Confirm the order, apply any visible coupon, and pay through the checkout before choosing the delivery method.

Submit account details

For Manual Top-Up, enter the linked account email and the Character ID of the Last Z character that should receive the purchase.

Finish delivery

With Self-Service Top-Up, log in to the game through LDShop; with Manual Top-Up, enter the email code. If a risk-control alert appears, stay on the page for 10 minutes and try again.

When LDShop Makes Sense

LDShop is most useful for planned purchases where the discount is visible before checkout: Gold Bars you already expect to spend, or an event or upgrade window where you know which pack you will buy with them in the game.

Self-Service Top-Up vs Manual Top-Up

Self-Service Top-Up

You pay on LDShop, then launch the game through LDShop and log in with email to complete the top-up.

  • Works with email login only; Apple account login is not supported.
  • Before you redeem a bundle this way, LDShop recommends spending Gold Bars in the game until your balance is lower than the bundle price.
  • LDShop logs you out automatically when the top-up is complete.

Manual Top-Up

You pay on LDShop, choose Manual Top-Up, enter the linked email and Character ID, then the verification code from your email. Track the order in LDShop's Order Center, then check in the game.

Before You Pay

Before paying, compare the price and confirm which account should receive the purchase.

  • The LDShop price beats the in-game or official-store route for the same item.
  • The product matches the Gold Bar amount or pack you want.
  • The account email and Game UID belong to the same Last Z character.
  • The account email is available for a verification code.
  • Your Gold Bar balance matches the way you want to buy the pack.
  • For Self-Service Top-Up, you can log in to the game with email; Apple account login is not supported.

FAQ

Is LDShop cheaper than the official Last Z store?

Yes. LDShop can be cheaper than the official store for Gold Bars and selected packs when the live discount is active.

What information do I need for a Last Z top-up?

For Manual Top-Up, the linked account email and the Character ID of the character that should receive the purchase, plus access to that email for the verification code. For Self-Service Top-Up, you log in to the game with email yourself.

Why can Gold Bars affect a pack purchase?

Last Z can use your Gold Bar balance before it charges cash for some packs. Example: if a pack is priced like a $19.99 purchase and your account already has enough Gold Bars, the game can spend those Gold Bars first.

The simpler route is to buy discounted Gold Bars first, then spend them on the pack inside Last Z. You still get the pack, but the value comes from the cheaper Gold Bar purchase.

What is Self-Service Top-Up?

You pay on LDShop, then launch the game through LDShop and log in with email to complete the top-up. Apple account login is not supported.

Is Manual Top-Up available?

Yes. On November 21, 2026, LDShop listed Manual Top-Up: enter the linked email and Character ID, then the email verification code.

Why does LDShop ask for a verification code?

Email verification connects the order to the correct account. Use the latest code from the email tied to the Last Z account.

Is this the same as redeeming codes?

No. Redeem codes use the Last Z Gift Center flow. LDShop top-up is a paid purchase flow for Gold Bars, packs, or selected offers.

Good To Know

  • Gold Bar and pack prices move with live LDShop offers, so the current page is the number that matters.
  • The account email and Game UID should point to the character that receives the top-up.
  • Redeem codes are separate from paid top-ups. For free codes, use the Last Z codes guide.
