Privacy Policy

Last Z Guides is a free fan site for Last Z: Survival Shooter. This page explains what data the site and its partners collect and what choices you have.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which guides and tools people use, so we can improve them. It records pages viewed, device type, browser and approximate location, and it sets cookies to do this. We use it only for aggregated statistics. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising

Some guides and the gift codes page show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can turn off personalised ads in Google's My Ad Center, and opt out of personalised ads from other vendors at aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see only limited ads, which are not personalised. Google may still use cookies or similar storage there to detect fraud and invalid clicks.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Data stored in your browser

Our planners and some calculators save your plans or settings in your browser's local storage, so they are still there next time. The site also remembers small settings, such as a message you closed. This data stays on your device, and clearing your browser data removes it. If you share a Research Planner link, the plan is included in the link, so the site receives it when someone opens the link.

Partner and support links

Some pages link to partners, such as LDShop, and to our Ko-fi page. Partner links are marked, and the sites they open have their own privacy policies. See our Disclosure.

Hosting

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Cookies

Analytics and ads use cookies as described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site still works without them. We do not sell personal data.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at lastzguides@gmail.com.
