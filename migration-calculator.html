Last Z Migration Score Calculator

Enter your four power values to estimate your Migration Score.

Use the values in Profile → Details. Enter full numbers or use M for millions: 20,000,000 and 20M mean the same value.

Use Hero Power, not Troop Power or total account power.

Estimated Migration Score

Enter all four power values to calculate your estimate.

Last Z does not disclose the exact formula. This community estimate is based on player examples from server 403

How the estimate works

Hero and Modified Vehicle Power have more weight than Structure and Tech Power.

It does not calculate separate bonuses for T10 or Destroyer unlocks. Both can have a major impact on your in-game Migration Score, so your actual score may be much higher than this estimate.
